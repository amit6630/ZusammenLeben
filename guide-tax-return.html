Taxes in Germany: The Basics for Employees

As soon as you work in Germany, income tax is deducted from your wage. Many employees can get part of it back with a tax return. Understanding a few basics saves money and avoids unpleasant letters from the tax office.

Updated: 28 October 20266 min readZusammenLeben

Your tax ID

After you register your address, the Federal Central Tax Office sends your personal tax identification number (Steuer-ID) by post. It has eleven digits and stays the same for life, even if you move. Every employer needs it to register you correctly. Keep the letter safe. If it is lost, you can request it again online or from the tax office (Finanzamt).

How income tax is deducted

Employees do not pay income tax themselves during the year. The employer deducts wage tax (Lohnsteuer) and, if applicable, the solidarity surcharge and church tax from your gross pay and transfers it to the tax office. The amount depends on your income and your tax class. Your monthly pay slip shows every deduction, together with social insurance contributions.

Tax classes

  • Class I: single, divorced, or married with the spouse living abroad.
  • Class II: single parents living alone with at least one child; includes a relief amount.
  • Classes III and V: married couples where one earns much more than the other; III for the higher earner.
  • Class IV: married couples with similar incomes, each in IV.
  • Class VI: for a second or further job.

Married couples are automatically placed in IV/IV when both are registered in Germany; they can apply for a change. Single parents should make sure they are in class II, because it reduces monthly tax.

Church tax: an important trap

At registration you are asked about your religion. If you state membership of the Roman Catholic or Protestant church, church tax is deducted from your wage, usually eight percent of the income tax in Bavaria. Members of the Ukrainian Greek Catholic Church may be counted as Catholic. Orthodox churches in Germany generally do not collect church tax. State your religion truthfully, but understand the consequence. Leaving a church is possible at the registry office or local court and ends church tax.

Is a tax return compulsory?

For many employees the tax return (Steuererklärung) is voluntary. It is compulsory in certain cases, for example:

  • if you had two or more employers at the same time (class VI);
  • if you are married and one partner is in class III or V;
  • if you received certain wage replacement benefits above a limit, such as short-time work allowance or parental allowance;
  • if you are self-employed or had other income above a limit;
  • if the tax office asks you to submit one.

Compulsory returns are due by the end of July of the following year, or later if a tax adviser prepares them.

Why a voluntary return is often worth it

Wage tax is calculated as if you worked the whole year with the same income. If you started work in the middle of the year, changed jobs or had expenses for work, too much tax was usually deducted. Many employees get a refund of several hundred euros. A voluntary return can be filed up to four years back.

What you can deduct

  • Commuting: a flat rate per kilometre between home and work, regardless of how you travel.
  • Work equipment: tools, work clothes, a computer used for work.
  • Training: job-related courses, language courses you paid yourself, books.
  • Job applications: photos, printing, postage, travel to interviews.
  • Childcare costs for children up to 14, partly deductible.
  • Donations to recognised charities, and trade union fees.

An automatic flat rate for work expenses applies anyway; your own costs only help if they exceed it. Keep receipts for one year after the decision.

Filing for free with ELSTER

ELSTER is the free online portal of the tax authorities. Registration takes a few days, because an activation code is sent by post to your address. Many data, such as your wage and taxes withheld, are already pre-filled by your employer. You enter additional expenses and submit. The tax office then sends a tax assessment (Steuerbescheid). Check it: if something is wrong, you can object within one month.

Tip: register for ELSTER early in the year. The activation letter can take up to two weeks, and you need it before you can start.

Help with your tax return

Income tax assistance associations (Lohnsteuerhilfevereine) prepare tax returns for employees for an annual membership fee based on income. Tax advisers are more expensive and mainly useful for self-employed people or complex cases. Some migration counselling services and volunteers help with simple returns.

Tax refunds and Jobcenter benefits

If you receive Jobcenter benefits when the refund arrives, it usually counts as income in that month and reduces benefits. Report it, as with all income. Plan with this in mind; it is not a reason to skip the return, especially if you no longer receive benefits by then.

Income from Ukraine

If you live in Germany, you are generally taxed here on your worldwide income, including income from Ukraine, for example from remote work or rent. Germany and Ukraine have a double taxation agreement that prevents paying tax twice on the same income. If you have such income, get advice from a tax adviser or assistance association.

Couples where one partner lives in Ukraine

If you are married but your spouse still lives in Ukraine, you are usually taxed in class I. In some cases, joint assessment with the tax benefits for married couples is possible even if the spouse lives abroad, but strict conditions apply, for example on the spouse's income and certificates from the Ukrainian tax authority. Ask a tax assistance association whether it is worth applying in your case.

Self-employed people: advance payments

Self-employed people pay income tax themselves through quarterly advance payments (Vorauszahlungen) set by the tax office, usually due in March, June, September and December. Put aside a share of every payment you receive for taxes, so that a high bill does not surprise you. If your income drops, you can apply to reduce the advance payments.

Keep a tax folder

A simple folder for each year saves stress: annual wage tax certificate (Lohnsteuerbescheinigung) from your employer, receipts for work expenses, childcare invoices, donation receipts and every letter from the tax office. Digital photos are often enough for the return, but keep the originals.

Letters from the tax office

Open every letter from the tax office and note deadlines. Common letters are the tax assessment, requests for documents and, for self-employed people, notices of advance payments. If you do not understand a letter, get help quickly; deadlines are strict.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to file a tax return?

Often not, but it is compulsory in some cases, for example with two jobs or tax classes III/V.

Is a voluntary return worth it?

Often yes. Many employees get money back, especially after starting work during the year.

How far back can I file?

A voluntary return can be filed up to four years back.

What is church tax?

A tax for members of the Catholic and Protestant churches, deducted with wage tax. It depends on the religion stated at registration.

Is ELSTER free?

Yes. ELSTER is the free official online portal of the tax authorities.
