How to Read and Answer Jobcenter Letters

Official letters in German are the biggest source of stress for the families we support. Most of them follow the same few patterns. Once you recognise the type of letter, you know what to do and how much time you have.

Updated: 26 October 20265 min readZusammenLeben

Why Jobcenter letters feel so difficult

Official German is hard even for Germans. Jobcenter letters use long legal sentences, refer to paragraphs of the Social Code (Sozialgesetzbuch, SGB II) and often arrive in several pages at once. The good news: almost every letter belongs to one of a few types, and each type has a clear next step. Once you know the type, you know what to do and how much time you have.

The five letters you will see most often

  • Bescheid (decision): approves, changes or refuses benefits. A Bewilligungsbescheid approves, an Änderungsbescheid changes amounts, an Ablehnungsbescheid refuses. Check the amounts, the period and the names of all family members.
  • Einladung (invitation): an appointment with your caseworker or the employment service. Missing it without a reason can lead to a reduction. If you are ill, send a doctor's certificate before or on the day.
  • Mitwirkungsaufforderung (request to cooperate): the Jobcenter needs documents, for example bank statements, a rental contract, pay slips or a school certificate. There is always a deadline, and benefits can be stopped if you do not react.
  • Anhörung (hearing): the Jobcenter thinks something is wrong, for example an overpayment or a missed appointment, and gives you the chance to explain before it decides. Answering is your best chance to avoid a negative decision.
  • Weiterbewilligungsantrag (renewal): benefits are approved for a limited period, usually twelve months. Before it ends you must apply again. Without the renewal, payments stop.

Find the key facts on the first page

  1. Date of the letter, usually top right. Deadlines are often counted from the day you receive it.
  2. Deadline: look for words like bis zum, Frist, spätestens or innerhalb von.
  3. Your file number: the BG-Nummer (number of your household) or Kundennummer. Write it on everything you send back.
  4. Contact person: name, phone number and email of the team responsible.
  5. What is required: often listed with bullet points or a checklist of documents.
Tip: write the deadline on the envelope and in your phone calendar the day the letter arrives. Most problems come from letters left unopened for a week.

How to answer correctly

Answer in writing, always with your file number, date and signature. You can submit documents in three ways: through the online portal of the Jobcenter (jobcenter.digital), by handing them in at reception, or by post. At reception, ask for a stamped copy (Eingangsbestätigung) as proof. Never send originals; send copies and keep the originals at home. If you need more time, ask for an extension in writing before the deadline and give a reason.

Disagree with a decision? Use an objection

At the end of each decision there is a section called Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung. It explains that you can file an objection (Widerspruch), usually within one month of receiving the letter. A short written objection with your file number, the date of the decision and the sentence "Hiermit lege ich Widerspruch gegen den Bescheid vom … ein" is enough to keep the deadline. You can add the reasons later.

An objection is free. The Jobcenter must check the decision again and send you a new decision (Widerspruchsbescheid). If you still disagree, you can go to the social court (Sozialgericht), which is also free for you. Independent advice is available from migration counselling services, social organisations such as Caritas or Diakonie, and lawyers; with a low income you can apply for legal advice assistance (Beratungshilfe) at the local court.

Sample objection (in German, as the Jobcenter expects it):

Vorname Nachname, Straße Hausnummer, PLZ Ort
BG-Nummer: …
An das Jobcenter …, Datum

Widerspruch gegen den Bescheid vom …
Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,
hiermit lege ich Widerspruch gegen den Bescheid vom … ein. Die Begründung reiche ich nach. Bitte bestätigen Sie den Eingang.
Mit freundlichen Grüßen, Unterschrift

Replace the dots with your details, sign it and keep a copy. Send it by post or hand it in at reception and ask for a stamped copy. Then collect your reasons calmly, ideally with an adviser, and send them within a few weeks.

Report changes straight away

A new job, a change in working hours, a move, a child starting school or turning 18, someone moving in or out, or money you receive from anyone else: all of these change your benefits. Report them as soon as they happen, ideally with a short letter and the relevant document. Reporting late is the most common reason for repayment demands (Erstattungsbescheid), and repayments are usually deducted from future benefits.

Words that appear in almost every letter

  • Leistungen: benefits, the money you receive for living costs and housing.
  • Regelbedarf: the standard monthly amount for food, clothing and daily needs.
  • Kosten der Unterkunft (KdU): housing costs, meaning rent, service charges and heating.
  • Bedarfsgemeinschaft: everyone in your household who is counted together for benefits.
  • Einkommen anrechnen: income is deducted from your benefits, in part or in full.
  • Nachweis: proof, any document that shows something is true.
  • Zugang: the day a letter is considered received; deadlines are counted from it.
  • Minderung: a reduction of benefits, for example after a missed appointment.

Appointments with your caseworker

At appointments the Jobcenter discusses your language course, job search and next steps. Bring your ID, the invitation letter and any new documents. You may bring a person you trust; if your German is not yet good enough, ask in advance whether an interpreter can be arranged or bring someone who can translate. Sometimes a written agreement on your next steps (Kooperationsplan) is made. Read it before you sign and ask about anything unclear.

Keep a simple file at home

A folder with clear sections saves hours: decisions, letters you sent, rental documents, pay slips, school and health documents. Keep everything for several years. Take a photo of each letter on the day it arrives, so you can send it to an adviser quickly.

How we help

Many families send us a photo of a letter on WhatsApp. We explain in Ukrainian, Russian, German or English what the letter means, what is required and by when, and help you prepare the answer. We do not give legal advice and do not decide anything for the Jobcenter, but we make sure you understand the process and arrive prepared. Ask for help.

Frequently asked questions

How long do I have to object to a Jobcenter decision?

Usually one month after you receive the decision. The exact rule is in the Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung at the end of the letter.

What if I cannot attend an appointment?

Tell the Jobcenter before the appointment and give the reason. If you are ill, send a doctor's certificate.

Does an objection cost money?

No. The objection procedure is free, and so is a case at the social court for you as a benefit recipient.

When must I apply for renewal?

Before your current approval period ends. The end date is written in your last decision; apply a few weeks earlier.

Can ZusammenLeben read my letters with me?

Yes. Send us a photo on WhatsApp and we explain what the letter asks and what the deadline is. We do not give legal advice.
