Emergency Guide & Support Services

Northern Bavaria — Phone Numbers, Addresses, and Assistance

A comprehensive index of emergency services, medical centers, legal aid, housing assistance, and social support networks in the region, structured for rapid access during a crisis.

Important Operational Clarification

Our social initiative is a volunteer-run support network and does not replace official emergency dispatch centers, the police, fire departments, hospitals, or the Jugendamt.

In any immediate life-threatening situation, severe accident, fire, or active violence — dial 112 or 110 immediately. We cannot guarantee 24/7 availability or instant resolution for every case.

Immediate Danger — Call Now

Use these numbers when seconds count. Free from any landline or mobile phone across Germany and the EU.

Life Safety 24/7 Free
112

Ambulance, Emergency Doctor, and Fire Department

Call for situations including:

  • Life-threatening medical emergencies & severe breathing difficulties
  • Loss of consciousness or severe accidents
  • Fires and serious injuries
  • Suspected poisoning & emergency doctor dispatch
Valid across all EU member states.
Law Enforcement 24/7 Free
110

Police Emergency Response

Call for situations including:

  • Active violence or threat of physical harm
  • Burglaries or crimes in progress
  • Immediate danger to a child or domestic abuse
  • Severe harassment, stalking, or security threats
No need to visit a station in an active emergency.

Urgent Medical & Poison Control

Non-Life Threatening

116 117 — On-Call Medical Service

For urgent medical issues outside regular clinic hours (evenings, nights, weekends):

  • High fever without immediate life risk
  • Severe pain requiring prompt evaluation
  • Locating an open on-call medical clinic (Bereitschaftspraxis)
In life-threatening cases, always dial 112.
Toxicology

Poison Control Center (Bavaria / Munich)

089 19240

Contact for ingestion or exposure to toxic substances, chemicals, or hazardous plants. Published by Klinikum Fichtelgebirge.

If unconscious or struggling to breathe, dial 112 first.

What to Say During an Emergency Call

Be prepared to answer the 5 Ws clearly and calmly:

1. Where? City, street, house number, floor, doorbell name.
2. What? Brief description of the incident.
3. Who? Number of people involved and categories (children, elderly).
4. Condition? Consciousness, breathing, injuries.
5. Wait? Do not hang up until the dispatcher tells you to.
Essential German Phrases for Non-German Speakers:
Ich spreche nur EnglischI only speak English
Ich spreche UkrainischI speak Ukrainian

Support for Women Facing Violence or Distress

Confidential 24/7 Free

Violence Against Women Hotline

116 016

National counseling service for domestic violence, stalking, sexual assault, and human trafficking (Hilfetelefon Gewalt gegen Frauen).

  • Completely anonymous & free of charge
  • Available in Ukrainian, English, and other languages
  • Direct referral to local safe houses and shelters
Maternity Support

Pregnant Women in Distress

0800 40 40 020

Confidential guidance for pregnant women facing financial, social, or personal crises without adequate support (Hilfetelefon Schwangere in Not).

Available 24/7 in multiple languages, free and anonymous.

Help for Children, Youth, and Parents

Immediate Child Protection Report

If a child is in immediate danger, neglected, or exposed to violence, contact the Police (110) or Ambulance (112) immediately. For non-urgent welfare checks, contact the local Jugendamt (Youth Welfare Office). You do not need to be a relative to report a genuine safety concern.

Youth Hotline (Nummer gegen Kummer)

116 111

For children and teenagers dealing with stress, family conflicts, school pressure, bullying, or emotional distress. Mon–Sat, 14:00–20:00. Free and anonymous.

Parents Hotline

0800 111 0550

Guidance for parents on family stress, parenting challenges, and child well-being. Mon/Wed/Fri 09:00–17:00, Tue/Thu 09:00–19:00.

Homelessness, Food Shortages & Utility Emergencies

Risk of Eviction or No Shelter Tonight

  • Contact your municipal Ordnungsamt or city administration immediately.
  • Outside business hours, contact the police at 110 if you have nowhere safe to stay with children.
  • Notify your Jobcenter case worker in writing immediately upon receiving an eviction notice. Do not wait.
  • Keep copies of your lease, notices, and communications.

Food Shortages & Basic Supplies

  • Contact the Jobcenter or Sozialamt if benefits payments were delayed.
  • Reach out to local food banks (Tafel), Caritas, or Diakonie for emergency provisions.
  • Bring identification, your Anmeldung certificate, and bank statements when visiting aid offices.

Utility Failures, Gas Leaks & Lost Documents

  • Gas Smell: Do not use light switches or open flames. Evacuate the building immediately and call 112.
  • Heating/Water Outage: Document the issue with photos, notify your landlord or property management in writing immediately.
  • Arzberg Water Hotline: For municipal water supply and pipe bursts in Arzberg: 09233 404-40.
  • Lost Documents: File a police report if stolen, block bank cards immediately, and contact the immigration office for residence permits.
