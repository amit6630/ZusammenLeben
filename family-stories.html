TESTIMONIALS & STORIES

Letters of Gratitude

Stories of support, community, and hope from families and individuals we accompanied in Northern Bavaria.

Driven by Human Values: At ZusammenLeben, we believe true help begins with people. All our work is done entirely on a voluntary basis, free of charge, out of social responsibility, humanity, respect, and mutual support.

Building a New Beginning in Northern Bavaria

Since the beginning of our initiative, we have been accompanying families and individuals who arrived from Ukraine to Northern Bavaria. We help them find housing, deal with local authorities, register children in schools and daycare, overcome language barriers, and rebuild their lives in Germany.

This social work brings us deep joy and purpose. Every family that moves into a secure home, every child who enters a new school, and every person who feels they have someone to turn to reminds us why we created ZusammenLeben.

Together We Can Do More

ZusammenLeben was built on the belief that everyone can contribute to making the world a better place. Even a small action can make a massive difference for someone starting over.

Family Mentorship

Volunteering to guide and support families through local administrative steps.

Translation

Providing translation support between Ukrainian, German, and English.

Housing & Moving

Offering apartments, donating furniture, or helping with logistics and transport.

Work Opportunities

Connecting job seekers with local employers and professional networks.

Community Partnership

Professional or local community collaborations to broaden support.

Essential Aid

Assisting children and families with basic needs and school supplies.
