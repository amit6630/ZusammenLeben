REGIONAL SPECIALIZATION

Northern Bavaria & Upper Franconia

Why we operate in a single dedicated region — and how this targeted approach delivers tangible stability for arriving families.

Depth Over Scale

We could easily claim that we operate across all of Germany. It would sound larger, but it would be far less effective in practice. Genuine, reliable guidance requires deep local familiarity: knowing the exact municipal clerk handling your file, which school has immediate capacity for Ukrainian-speaking children, which property management company is willing to work directly with the Jobcenter, and where actual vacant apartments exist right now.

What Our Local Focus Enables

Municipal Authorities

We know how municipal offices operate in each town, their specific requirements, and realistic processing timelines.

Landlords & Property Managers

Relationships built over years. Landlords who know us respond faster and are willing to enter an unfamiliar intake process.

Local Jobcenter Operations

Accumulated experience regarding regional Jobcenter expectations, required documents, and approval standards.

Educational Institutions

We know which schools and kindergartens accept new children and where dedicated language support classes are active.

Existing Community

New families arrive in an area where others have already successfully walked the exact same path.

In-Person Support

Real physical presence — we can meet you locally, accompany you to official appointments, and inspect apartments in person.

Key Municipalities in Our Operating Area

Helmbrechts Main Hub

A medium-sized town with essential local services and reasonable housing availability. A major hub of our activity.

Münchberg

A location with good rail and road connectivity, local educational institutions, and resident services.

Hof Regional Center

The largest city in the area. Highest concentration of authorities and services, diverse schools, comprehensive healthcare, and public transit.

Selb

An industrial town with a history of integrating workers, local services, and available housing stock.

Wunsiedel

A district town hosting local government authorities and regional services.

Arzberg

A smaller settlement with a quiet residential character and housing availability that varies over time.

Marktredwitz

A regional junction with solid commercial options, services, and transportation links.

Naila

A town offering local services and educational institutions in a relatively rural atmosphere.

Kulmbach

A city with local authorities, educational and medical services, and established infrastructure.

Bad Steben

A small resort and health-spa town, quiet and more limited in general services.

* And additional surrounding towns and communities across Northern Bavaria and Upper Franconia based on need and availability.

What to Know About the Region

BENEFITS

Clear Advantages

This is a rural-urban region, not a metropolis. The benefits are clear: lower housing costs than in major cities, a quiet environment, smaller educational institutions, and less administrative overload.

EXPECTATIONS

Factors to Keep in Mind

Public transport in smaller towns runs less frequently than in big cities, job opportunities are more limited, and travel to a neighboring city may be required for certain services. Families arriving from Kyiv or Kharkiv often experience a change in pace. We state this upfront to ensure accurate expectations.

Geographic Scope Clarification

Our operational support does not extend fully across all of Germany. Inquiries from other regions will be reviewed, but in most cases, we cannot provide practical assistance outside our primary area. Where possible, we will try to refer you to a relevant local contact.
